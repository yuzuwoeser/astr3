### 1. Astro Caster

Short connection note:

> I saw Astro Caster's natal/transit UI. I'm sharing ASTR3, an MIT TypeScript wheel
> with selection events for application panels. Would you be open to comparing the
> interaction for a small web experiment?

If a normal message is available:

> Astro Caster's chart and timing panels look like a relevant place to test
> selection-driven rendering. ASTR3 can render positions you already compute and
> expose the selected object's immediate relationships to your panel. It covers
> natal/transit wheels, not your whole feature set. Here's a small invented-data
> demo: https://yuzuwoeser.github.io/astr3/examples/selection/
> Would an optional rendering experiment be useful, or is your current wheel settled?
