# Community research

Primary sources checked on 2026-10-25. Recheck before posting. The three candidates
below have different writing requirements; generated copy is not suitable for
every community. No submissions or messages have been made.

## 1. Three.js forum, Showcase: first choice

- Destination: [Showcase](https://discourse.threejs.org/c/showcase/7).
- The [category description](https://discourse.threejs.org/t/about-the-showcase-category/25)
  invites projects made with Three.js and says posts require moderator approval.
- The [forum guidelines](https://discourse.threejs.org/guidelines) prohibit spam,
  duplicate cross-posts and derailing discussions. No explicit AI-writing rule was
  found on those reviewed pages; this is not a guarantee of moderator approval.
- Use the technical showcase draft in [launch-copy.md](launch-copy.md), with a
  screenshot, demo and source. Keep the AI-assistance disclosure and personally
  review the claims. Discuss picking, labels, selection and lifecycle when asked.
- Fit: rendering feedback and technically relevant visibility. Direct astrology
  app adoption is an inference, not a known property of this audience.

## 2. r/webdev, Showoff Saturday: human-written only

- Destination and current rules: [r/webdev](https://www.reddit.com/r/webdev/).
- Rule 5 limits project showcases and feedback requests to Saturday, requires the
  correct flair and a technical focus. Rule 4 prohibits commercial promotion.
- Rule 8 expressly includes LLM-generated posts and comments among disallowed
  low-effort content. Rule 3 also discourages excessive self-promotion.
- Use only if your existing account has an appropriate participation history,
  the post is a technical project showcase, and you write it yourself. Do not
  paste or lightly rewrite generated launch copy or generated replies.
- Factual material to inspect: demo, README, renderer source, bounded label
  packing and React cleanup. Choose what you personally want to discuss.

## 3. Hacker News, Show HN: optional, human-written only

- Destination guidance: [Show HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html).
- Submit the working demo when it is live and usable without an account; a
  landing page, tutorial article or signup form does not meet the stated format.
- The [HN guidelines](https://news.ycombinator.com/newsguidelines.html) prohibit
  generated or AI-edited posts/comments, automated posting and soliciting votes.
  They also ask users not to use the site primarily for promotion.
- No ready-to-paste HN post is supplied. Write your own explanation after trying
  the demo and reviewing the implementation. Be available to discuss your work.
- Lower priority than direct integration conversations. This is a technical
  showcase opportunity, not evidence that the audience needs astrology software.

## Considered but excluded from the launch sequence

**DEV:** its [AI-assisted article policy](https://dev.to/guidelines-for-ai-assisted-articles-on-dev)
requires disclosure and fact checking, but also says assisted/generated articles
should not promote a program or primarily build a brand. It prohibits generated
comments except narrow assistance cases. A promotional ASTR3 draft is therefore
not an appropriate use of that allowance. Publish the tutorial on the project
website instead.

**r/SideProject:** public project posts were visible, but the dedicated rules page
did not expose sufficient policy text to verify promotion and assisted-writing
requirements. Treat it as an unqualified backup, not a ready posting destination.

## Posting order

Publish the verified website changes first. Share the short announcement on an
existing personal account, then submit one Three.js showcase. Start relevant
individual conversations. Consider either human-written channel only if you want
to participate there; both are optional. Do not coordinate votes, mass cross-post,
or repost a moderated submission while waiting for approval.
